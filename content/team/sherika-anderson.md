## About Me

I am dedicated to supporting individuals, couples, and families as they navigate life's challenges and relational concerns. I know that starting therapy can feel intimidating, so my goal is to create a safe, supportive, and judgment-free space where you feel genuinely heard and understood. I believe clients are the experts of their own lives and my role is to partner with you to build insight, strengthen your relationships, and work toward meaningful, sustainable change.

## Clinical Focus & Specializations

I work with individuals, couples, and families across different life stages and experiences, with a focus on:

- **Primary Specialty:** Relational concerns, couples counseling, and family systems navigating life transitions.
- **Family & Couples Dynamics:** Understanding how patterns of interaction, human development, and family background impact emotional well-being.
- **Individual Support:** Navigating personal life challenges, self-insight, and emotional growth.
- **Relational Health:** Strengthening communication, resolving conflicts, and building deeper connections.

## My Approach

Therapy relies on a strong, collaborative partnership. Here is what you can expect from working with me:

- **Relational & Systems-Based:** I look at the big picture and recognize how family dynamics, life experiences, and interaction patterns shape your emotional health.
- **Client-Centered Collaboration:** Grounded in respect and compassion, I honor your lived experience and tailor our work to your unique goals.
- **Ethical & Reflective Care:** Committed to ongoing learning, clinical supervision, and reflective practice to offer you the best possible care.
