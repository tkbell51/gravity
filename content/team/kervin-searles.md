## About Me

I love being a therapist. I strive to create a nonjudgmental, safe, and transformative space where you can share your experience freely. My goal isn't to "fix" you, but to empower you with concrete skills to navigate life's challenges, find balance, and become your healthiest self.

## Clinical Focus & Specializations

I work with adults and adolescents navigating a wide range of mental health needs, including:

**Primary Specialty:**

- Anxiety, Depression, & Trauma
- Anger Management & Stress Relief
- Self-Esteem & Personal Growth
- Relationship & Family Dynamics

## My Approach

Therapy relies on a strong relationship. Here is what you can expect from me:

- **Plain Talk:** I speak in clear, layman's terms and little clinical jargon.
- **Tailored Care:** Utilizing Adlerian and ACT principles, I tailor evidence-based methods to your unique story.
- **Empowerment:** I offer insight and options, but you retain full ownership of your choices and change.

## Background & Experience

As managing partner of Gravity Counseling, my work spans individual, family, and group therapy, alongside workshops on anger management, parenting, and mental health.

- **Education:** MS in Counseling Psychology (Troy University) | BA in Psychology (Anderson University)
- **Credentials:** LPC & LPCS-Candidate (South Carolina)
