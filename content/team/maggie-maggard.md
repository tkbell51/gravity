## About Me

I am a caring clinician who believes everyone deserves to feel heard, supported, and respected. I strive to create a safe, welcoming, and nonjudgmental space where you can speak openly without fear of judgment. My approach is client-centered and collaborative, focusing on your unique experiences and personal strengths. I believe healing happens through genuine connection, trust, and small, meaningful steps forward. My goal is to walk alongside you as you build practical coping skills, find clarity, and move toward greater emotional well-being.

## Clinical Focus & Specializations

I work with individuals and families navigating a wide range of life challenges, including:

- Anxiety & Stress Relief
- Grief & Loss
- Difficult Life Changes & Transitions
- Coping Skills & Personal Growth
- Family Dynamics & Preservation

## My Approach

Therapy relies on trust, advocacy, and collaboration. Here is what you can expect from me:

- **Client-Centered Care:** I focus on your individual journey and build upon your innate strengths.
- **Safe & Welcoming Space:** I provide a compassionate environment where you can talk openly without judgment.
- **Collaborative Support:** I walk alongside you through challenging seasons of life, helping you take small, meaningful steps forward.
- **Advocacy & Empowerment:** I am passionate about supporting you, helping you develop concrete coping strategies and achieve emotional clarity.

## Background & Experience

My background includes experience in mental health counseling, family preservation, case management, and education.

- **Education:** Master's in Counseling Psychology (Southern Wesleyan University) | BS in Human Services (Lander University) | AS in Science and Art (Piedmont Technical College)
