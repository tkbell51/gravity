## About Me

I am dedicated to walking alongside individuals as they navigate life's challenges and work toward personal growth. My goal is to cultivate a supportive, compassionate, and collaborative environment where you feel truly heard, valued, and empowered to build meaningful coping skills and resilience.

## Clinical Focus & Specializations

I support youth, adults, and families through a variety of life transitions and mental health needs, including:

- Youth & Student Mental Health
- Anxiety, Stress & Coping Skill Development
- Personal Growth, Insight & Self-Esteem
- Life Transitions & Relational Dynamics

## My Approach

I believe a strong therapeutic alliance is the foundation for lasting change. When working with me, you can expect:

- **Compassionate Care:** A warm, safe, and nonjudgmental environment focused entirely on your unique goals.
- **Client-Centered Collaboration:** Evidence-based therapeutic techniques tailored specifically to your personal story and strengths.
- **Empowerment & Growth:** Guidance that emphasizes self-discovery, skill-building, and lasting personal development.
